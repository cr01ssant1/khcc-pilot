About the club

More than a game

Keymer & Hassocks Cricket Club has been at the heart of Hassocks and Keymer since 1896 – and 2026 marks our 130th anniversary. Our home is Adastra Park, with a pavilion and bar, changing rooms and a defibrillator on site, and we also play at Clayton Recreation Ground.

Whether you're picking up a bat for the first time, returning after a few years away, or looking for competitive league cricket, there's a place for you here.

  • Saturday cricket in the Sussex Cricket League
  • Juniors in the South Area Young Cricketers League & Sussex Junior Cricket Festival
  • Welcoming, inclusive and family-friendly
